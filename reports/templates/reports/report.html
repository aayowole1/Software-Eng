How numbers are calculated

  • Attendance % = events attended ÷ events held in range × 100
  • Committee attendance % = total attendances by committee members ÷ (members × events held) × 100
  • Overdue tasks = tasks with a past due date that are not yet Done, as of today
  • Cancelled events are excluded. Only events whose start date falls within the selected range are counted.
  • Only tasks created within the selected date range are counted.
