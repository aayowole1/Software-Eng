How numbers are calculated
  • Attendance % = events attended ÷ events held in range since the member's membership started × 100
  • Committee attendance % = total attendances by committee members ÷ the events each member could attend since their membership started × 100
  • A member's membership starts when their application was last approved (or when the account was created, for accounts added without an application).
  • Overdue tasks = tasks with a past due date that are not yet Done, as of today
  • Events held are non-cancelled events that started within the selected range and have already taken place. Cancelled and upcoming events are not counted.
  • Only tasks created within the selected date range are counted.
  • Dates use the organization's time zone and include the whole start and end day.
