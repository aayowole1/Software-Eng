Our accessibility commitment

OrgFlow aims to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at the AA level. We work toward content that is perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust across devices and assistive technologies.

What we prioritize

  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Meaningful headings, labels, link text, and alternative text.
  • Readable contrast in light and dark themes.
  • Responsive layouts that remain usable when zoomed or resized.
  • Clear status messages and instructions for important actions.

Need an accommodation or found a barrier?

We want to hear from you. Contact an OrgFlow officer through your organization or the contact method provided on the dashboard. Please include the page, task, and assistive technology or browser involved when possible.

Our governance principles

OrgFlow is a student organization, and our governance is grounded in service, transparency, and shared responsibility. Officers steward the platform and help set priorities; members contribute feedback and shape how the community grows.

How decisions work

  1. Listen first. We gather input from the organizations and students who use OrgFlow.
  2. Document decisions. Significant operational and policy decisions should have a clear owner and an accessible record.
  3. Use least privilege. Access to member and organization information is limited to what a role needs.
  4. Plan for continuity. Processes and knowledge should be transferable when student leaders change.

Community expectations

We expect participants to communicate respectfully, protect private information, resolve disagreements in good faith, and use OrgFlow for legitimate organization work. Officers may review access or activity when needed to keep the community safe and operational.

Questions about governance can be raised with an OrgFlow officer.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use OrgFlow, we may handle account details, membership applications, organization activity, event responses, tasks, and messages you choose to share. We use this information to provide the platform, support organization operations, maintain security, and improve the student experience.

Our privacy practices

  • We request information relevant to the feature being used.
  • We use role-based access controls to limit administrative visibility.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We retain information only as long as it supports operational, safety, or recordkeeping needs.
  • We investigate suspected misuse and take reasonable steps to protect the service.

Questions about your information?

Contact an OrgFlow officer to ask about your account, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before discussing account-specific information.
